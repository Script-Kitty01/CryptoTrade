        CHRONOTRUST — FINAL YEAR PROJECT DOCUMENT
  Temporal Causal Inference for Crypto Purchase Intent Verification
================================================================


----------------------------------------------------------------
1. PROJECT TITLE
----------------------------------------------------------------

ChronoTrust: A Framework for Measuring Manipulation Exposure
in Cryptocurrency Purchase Decisions Using Temporal Causal Inference


----------------------------------------------------------------
2. AIM
----------------------------------------------------------------

To design and develop an end-to-end web application that detects
coordinated market manipulation campaigns in cryptocurrency markets
and quantifies the degree to which an individual user's purchase
intent was causally influenced by those campaigns — producing a
Manipulation Exposure Score (0–100) backed by temporal causal
analysis.


----------------------------------------------------------------
3. PROBLEM STATEMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------

Cryptocurrency markets are frequently targeted by coordinated
pump-and-dump schemes orchestrated through social media platforms.
Existing research detects manipulation at the market level, after
the fact. No existing system traces the causal chain from a
manipulation event all the way to an individual user's purchase
decision and measures their exposure to that manipulation in
real time.

This project addresses that gap.


----------------------------------------------------------------
4. RESEARCH QUESTION
----------------------------------------------------------------

Can a user's cryptocurrency purchase intent be quantifiably linked
to upstream manipulation events using temporal causal analysis,
and can this linkage be measured and presented in real time as
an actionable risk score?


----------------------------------------------------------------
5. OBJECTIVES
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. Continuously ingest real-time price, social media, news, and
   on-chain data from multiple sources.

2. Detect coordinated inauthentic behavior in social media posts
   using account clustering and behavioral pattern analysis.

3. Apply Granger Causality testing to determine whether social
   activity causally precedes price movements (not the reverse).

4. Build a causal influence graph linking social events to price
   movements using a graph database.

5. Track user activity within the application and cross-reference
   it against detected manipulation events.

6. Generate a Manipulation Exposure Score for each purchase intent
   action with a full evidence breakdown.

7. Validate the score against historical pump-and-dump events
   where outcomes are known.


----------------------------------------------------------------
6. SCOPE
----------------------------------------------------------------

- Covers Bitcoin, Ethereum, Dogecoin, and top 20 coins by market cap
- Data sources: Reddit, CryptoPanic news aggregator, Whale Alert
- Exposure window: 48-hour lookback from point of purchase intent
- Users must be authenticated to receive a score
- The system advises, it does not execute trades

9. MANIPULATION EXPOSURE SCORE — EXPLAINED
----------------------------------------------------------------

When a user clicks "Analyze Purchase Intent" on a coin, the system:

Step 1 — Looks back 48 hours in TimescaleDB for that coin's signals
Step 2 — Checks if any manipulation events fired (coordinated posts,
          whale movements, news spikes)
Step 3 — Runs Granger Causality test: did social activity cause the
          price move, or did price move cause social activity?
Step 4 — Cross-references with the user's in-app activity log to
          determine if they were active during or after the signal
Step 5 — Outputs a score with full evidence breakdown

Example Output:
  Coin: DOGE
  Score: 78 / 100 — HIGH RISK
  - 47 coordinated Reddit posts detected 16 hours before your session
  - 89% of posting accounts created within 2 weeks
  - Granger causality confirmed: posts caused price movement (p < 0.05)
  - No organic market driver found
  - Verdict: High probability your decision was shaped by a
    coordinated campaign. Recommended: wait 48 hours and reassess.


----------------------------------------------------------------
10. RESEARCH CONTRIBUTION
----------------------------------------------------------------

Existing work detects manipulation at the market level.
This project is the first end-to-end system to:

  a) Trace the causal chain from a social manipulation event
     all the way to an individual user's purchase decision

  b) Quantify that individual's exposure using temporal causal
     analysis (Granger Causality)

  c) Present the result as a real-time actionable score with
     interpretable evidence

This sits at the intersection of:
  - Causal inference (not just correlation)
  - Information provenance tracking
  - Behavioral economics in digital markets
  - Real-time financial fraud detection

11. EVALUATION CRITERIA
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. Technical Completeness
   - All system components functional end-to-end
   - Real-time data ingestion working
   - Score generated within acceptable latency

2. Research Validity
   - Granger Causality tests statistically sound (p-value reporting)
   - Backtested against minimum 10 known historical pump-and-dump events
   - Score correlation with actual bad outcomes measured

3. Novelty
   - Individual exposure measurement not present in existing literature
   - Multi-source temporal causal framework is original contribution

4. System Design
   - Justified use of TimescaleDB for time-series
   - Justified use of Neo4j for causal graph structure
   - Scalable ingestion pipeline via Celery

5. Documentation & Reproducibility
   - Docker Compose setup for full local reproduction
   - Research methodology clearly documented
   - Dataset of manipulation events published with project
